FAQ

Questions Parents Ask

Everything you need to know before choosing Ravion Pharma for your child.

What age group is Pedivion DROP suitable for?
Pedivion DROP is formulated and safe for children aged 0 to 12 years. The gentle syrupy base and precise 1 ml dropper make it ideal even for infants. Always consult your pediatrician for dosing guidance for children under 1 year.
Is PrimeDHA suitable for vegetarian/vegan families?
Yes! PrimeDHA uses 100% plant-based Algal DHA — derived from microalgae, not fish. This makes it completely fish-free, odour-free, and suitable for vegetarian and vegan families while delivering the same high-quality DHA that fish get from algae.
Can I give both Pedivion DROP and PrimeDHA together?
Yes, both products can be given together as they are complementary. Pedivion covers your child's complete multivitamin and mineral needs, while PrimeDHA focuses specifically on brain development through DHA and bone health through Vitamin D3. However, always consult your healthcare practitioner.
How many doses are in one 15 ml bottle?
Each 15 ml bottle provides exactly 15 servings at the recommended dose of 1 ml per day — a full 15-day supply. The precision glass dropper ensures accurate dosing every time.
Are these products medicines or supplements?
Both Pedivion DROP and PrimeDHA are FSSAI-approved nutraceutical health supplements — NOT medicines. They are not intended to treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent any specific disease, disorder, or condition. They support overall nutritional status in growing children.
How should I store these drops?
Store in a cool, dry, and dark place. Protect from direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children. Both products have a shelf life of 18 months from the date of manufacturing. Refrigeration is not required but helps maintain freshness after opening.
